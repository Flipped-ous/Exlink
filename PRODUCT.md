# EXLINK Studio

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
React, TypeScript, Vite, Electron on Windows; uPlot and a virtualized log list.

## Users
Embedded developers using the planned EXLINK PRO multifunction debugger for daily bench debugging.

## Product Purpose
Connect a debugging session, send commands, read raw logs, and inspect multiple numeric channels together.

## Capabilities and Constraints
This iteration builds desktop UI with local demo and JSONL data. Hardware, firmware, real serial acquisition and power control are deferred. Tools: serial, logic, power, toolbox; settings at the bottom of the left navigation. SWD and auxiliary measurement are excluded. Serial waveforms collapse without stopping ingest. Up to 32 decoded numeric channels. No node editor, waveform-height slider, or Tabbit use.

## Brand Commitments
Keep the user-approved purple folded-ribbon E image. Use expressive Liquid Glass and fluid-cloud surfaces. Dark first, companion light theme. Left icon-and-text navigation about 110px; serial log/waveform split about 60/40; narrow windows stack. Customization lives in settings.

## Evidence on Hand
The approved brand reference is design/exlink-brand-reference-v3.png and the generated icon is public/brand/exlink-ribbon-dark-v3.png. Existing parsers, buffered session data and JSONL are real local functionality; hardware connection is not implemented.

## Product Principles
Preserve original bytes. Distinguish demonstration from hardware. Keep data readable while making material and interaction polished. Make settings predictable and support keyboard use.
